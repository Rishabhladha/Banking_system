Core Features

Built for Production,
Designed for People

Every feature is engineered to industry standard, from the database layer up.

Double-Entry Ledger

Every transaction creates both a DEBIT and CREDIT ledger entry. Balance is computed from aggregation — no stale data ever.

ACID Transactions

MongoDB multi-document sessions ensure your money transfers are atomic — either everything succeeds or everything rolls back.

Idempotent Transfers

Every transfer carries a unique idempotency key — retrying a failed transfer never charges you twice. Zero duplicate risk.

JWT + Token Blacklist

Secure JWT authentication with a TTL-indexed blacklist ensures logged-out tokens are always revoked. CSRF and XSS protected cookies.

Email Notifications

Instant email alerts on registration and every transaction via OAuth2-secured Gmail — fully async, never blocking your response.

Role-Based Access

System users have elevated privileges to fund accounts. Regular users can only access their own accounts — proper RBAC.
